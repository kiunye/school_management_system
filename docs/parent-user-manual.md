## Getting Started

### Receiving Your Account

When your child is enrolled in the school, you will automatically receive:
1. **Welcome Email**: Contains your login credentials
2. **SMS Notification**: Confirms account creation
3. **Login Instructions**: How to access the parent portal

### First Time Login

1. **Visit the School Website**: Go to your school's website
2. **Access Parent Portal**: Click on "Parent Login" or go to `yourschool.com/wp-admin`
3. **Enter Credentials**: Use the username and password from your welcome email
4. **Change Password**: You'll be prompted to create a new, secure password
5. **Complete Profile**: Add or verify your contact information

### Understanding Your Access

As a parent, you have access to:
- **Your Child's Academic Records**: Grades, attendance, and progress reports
- **Financial Information**: Fee statements, payment history, and outstanding balances
- **Communication Tools**: Messages from teachers and school administration
- **School Notices**: Important announcements and updates
- **Payment Portal**: Secure online fee payment system
- **Transport Information**: Route details and schedules (if applicable)

**Important**: You can only view information related to your own child(ren). All data is secure and private.

## Your Child's Information

### Viewing Child Profile

#### Personal Information
1. Navigate to **My Child > Profile**
2. View your child's details:
   - **Full Name**: As registered with the school
   - **Admission Number**: Unique student identifier
   - **Date of Birth**: Registered birth date
   - **Grade Level**: Current academic grade
   - **Class**: Current class and class teacher
   - **Academic Year**: Current academic session

#### Academic Information
- **Current Subjects**: All subjects your child is studying
- **Class Teacher**: Primary teacher contact information
- **Subject Teachers**: Teachers for specific subjects
- **Timetable**: Your child's weekly schedule

#### Contact Information
- **Your Details**: Your registered contact information
- **Emergency Contacts**: Alternative contact persons
- **Address**: Current residential address
- **Medical Information**: Allergies and medical conditions (if provided)

### Updating Information

#### What You Can Update
- **Your Contact Details**: Phone numbers, email address
- **Emergency Contacts**: Alternative contact persons
- **Address Information**: If you move to a new location
- **Medical Updates**: New allergies or medical conditions

#### How to Update Information
1. Go to **My Child > Profile**
2. Click **Edit Information**
3. Update the relevant fields
4. Click **Save Changes**
5. Changes are reviewed by school administration

**Note**: Some information like your child's name or admission number can only be changed by school administrators.

### Understanding the Grading System

#### Grade Scale
Your school uses the following grading system:
- **A (80-100%)**: Excellent performance
- **B (70-79%)**: Good performance
- **C (60-69%)**: Satisfactory performance
- **D (50-59%)**: Below average, needs improvement
- **E (Below 50%)**: Poor performance, requires intervention

#### Assessment Types
- **Continuous Assessment**: Regular class tests and assignments
- **Mid-term Exams**: Formal examinations mid-term
- **End-term Exams**: Final examinations at term end
- **Projects**: Special assignments and presentations

## Attendance Monitoring

### Daily Attendance

#### Viewing Attendance Records
1. Go to **Attendance > Daily Records**
2. View your child's attendance:
   - **Today's Status**: Whether your child is present today
   - **Weekly Summary**: Attendance for the current week
   - **Monthly Overview**: Attendance patterns for the month
   - **Term Summary**: Overall attendance for the current term

#### Attendance Status Indicators
- **✓ Present**: Your child was in school
- **✗ Absent**: Your child was not in school
- **L Late**: Your child arrived late
- **E Excused**: Authorized absence (medical, family emergency)

### Attendance Notifications

#### Automatic Alerts
You receive immediate notifications when:
- Your child is marked absent
- Your child arrives late to school
- Your child leaves school early
- There are attendance concerns

#### Notification Methods
- **SMS**: Instant text message to your phone
- **Email**: Detailed email notification
- **App Notification**: If using the mobile app

### Reporting Absences

#### Planned Absences
1. Navigate to **Attendance > Report Absence**
2. Select the date(s) your child will be absent
3. Choose the reason:
   - Medical appointment
   - Family emergency
   - Religious observance
   - Other (specify)
4. Upload supporting documents if required
5. Submit the absence report

#### Emergency Absences
For unexpected absences:
1. Call the school office immediately
2. Send SMS to the school number
3. Use the emergency absence reporting feature
4. Follow up with documentation when possible

### Attendance Analysis

#### Attendance Patterns
1. Go to **Attendance > Analysis**
2. View detailed patterns:
   - **Attendance Percentage**: Overall attendance rate
   - **Absence Trends**: Patterns in absences
   - **Punctuality Record**: Late arrival frequency
   - **Comparison**: How your child's attendance compares to class average

#### Attendance Concerns
If your child's attendance falls below school standards:
- You'll receive automated alerts
- The school may schedule a meeting
- Support resources will be provided
- Improvement plans may be developed

## Fee Management & Payments

### Understanding Your Fees

#### Fee Structure
1. Navigate to **Fees > Fee Structure**
2. View all applicable fees:
   - **Tuition Fees**: Academic instruction costs
   - **Activity Fees**: Sports, clubs, and extracurricular activities
   - **Transport Fees**: School bus services (if applicable)
   - **Meal Fees**: School lunch program (if applicable)
   - **Examination Fees**: Assessment and certification costs

#### Payment Schedule
- **Term-based Payments**: Fees due at the beginning of each term
- **Monthly Installments**: Spread payments throughout the term
- **Annual Payments**: Full year payment with possible discounts

### Viewing Your Account

#### Current Balance
1. Go to **Fees > Account Summary**
2. View your financial status:
   - **Outstanding Balance**: Amount currently due
   - **Paid Amount**: Payments made this term
   - **Next Due Date**: When next payment is required
   - **Payment History**: Record of all previous payments

#### Invoice Details
1. Click on any invoice to view:
   - **Invoice Number**: Unique reference number
   - **Due Date**: Payment deadline
   - **Fee Breakdown**: Detailed list of charges
   - **Payment Instructions**: How to make payment
   - **Late Fees**: Penalties for overdue payments (if applicable)

### Making Payments

#### Online Payment Options

##### M-Pesa Payment
1. Go to **Fees > Make Payment**
2. Select **M-Pesa** as payment method
3. Enter the amount to pay
4. Enter your M-Pesa phone number
5. Click **Pay Now**
6. Complete the payment on your phone when prompted
7. Receive confirmation SMS and email

##### Airtel Money Payment
1. Select **Airtel Money** as payment method
2. Enter payment amount and phone number
3. Follow the prompts to complete payment
4. Receive payment confirmation

##### Bank Transfer
1. Select **Bank Transfer** option
2. View bank account details
3. Make transfer using your bank's services
4. Upload proof of payment
5. Wait for verification by school finance office

#### Payment Confirmation
After successful payment:
- **Immediate Confirmation**: On-screen confirmation message
- **SMS Receipt**: Text message with payment details
- **Email Receipt**: Detailed email receipt
- **Account Update**: Your balance is updated immediately

### Payment History

#### Viewing Past Payments
1. Navigate to **Fees > Payment History**
2. View all your payments:
   - **Payment Date**: When payment was made
   - **Amount Paid**: Payment amount
   - **Payment Method**: How payment was made
   - **Reference Number**: Transaction reference
   - **Receipt**: Download or print receipt

#### Downloading Receipts
1. Click on any payment in your history
2. Select **Download Receipt**
3. Choose format (PDF recommended)
4. Save or print the receipt for your records

### Payment Reminders

#### Automatic Reminders
You receive payment reminders:
- **7 Days Before**: Due date reminder
- **3 Days Before**: Urgent reminder
- **On Due Date**: Final reminder
- **After Due Date**: Overdue notice

#### Setting Payment Preferences
1. Go to **Account > Notification Preferences**
2. Choose how you want to receive reminders:
   - SMS notifications
   - Email notifications
   - Both SMS and email

## Communication

### Messaging System

#### Receiving Messages
1. Navigate to **Messages > Inbox**
2. View messages from:
   - **Class Teachers**: Academic updates and behavioral feedback
   - **Subject Teachers**: Subject-specific information
   - **School Administration**: Official communications
   - **Finance Office**: Payment-related messages

#### Reading Messages
1. Click on any message to open it
2. View message details:
   - **Sender**: Who sent the message
   - **Date and Time**: When it was sent
   - **Subject**: Message topic
   - **Content**: Full message text
   - **Attachments**: Any files included

#### Replying to Messages
1. Open the message you want to reply to
2. Click **Reply**
3. Type your response
4. Click **Send Reply**

**Note**: Keep replies professional and concise. Teachers appreciate clear, specific communication.

### Sending Messages

#### Contacting Teachers
1. Go to **Messages > New Message**
2. Select the recipient:
   - **Class Teacher**: For general academic or behavioral concerns
   - **Subject Teacher**: For specific subject-related questions
   - **School Administration**: For administrative matters

3. Choose message type:
   - **Academic Inquiry**: Questions about your child's performance
   - **Absence Notification**: Reporting planned absences
   - **General Communication**: Other school-related matters

4. Write your message clearly and politely
5. Click **Send Message**

#### Message Guidelines
- **Be Specific**: Clearly state your concern or question
- **Be Respectful**: Use polite, professional language
- **Be Patient**: Allow 24-48 hours for responses
- **Be Relevant**: Keep messages school-related

### Parent-Teacher Conferences

#### Scheduling Meetings
1. Navigate to **Communication > Schedule Meeting**
2. Select the teacher you want to meet
3. Choose available time slots
4. Specify the purpose of the meeting
5. Confirm the appointment

#### Virtual Meetings
If your school offers virtual meetings:
1. You'll receive meeting link via email
2. Test your device and internet connection beforehand
3. Join the meeting at the scheduled time
4. Have your questions prepared in advance

## Troubleshooting

### Common Issues

#### Cannot Log In
**Possible Causes:**
- Incorrect username or password
- Account locked due to multiple failed attempts
- Browser issues or cached data

**Solutions:**
1. **Check Credentials**: Verify username and password
2. **Reset Password**: Use "Forgot Password" link
3. **Clear Browser Cache**: Clear cookies and cached data
4. **Try Different Browser**: Use alternative browser
5. **Contact School**: Call administration if problem persists

#### Payment Not Processing
**Possible Causes:**
- Insufficient funds in mobile money account
- Network connectivity issues
- Payment gateway maintenance

**Solutions:**
1. **Check Balance**: Ensure sufficient funds in your account
2. **Retry Payment**: Try the payment again after a few minutes
3. **Alternative Method**: Use different payment method
4. **Contact Support**: Call school finance office

#### Not Receiving Notifications
**Possible Causes:**
- Incorrect phone number or email
- Network issues
- Notification settings disabled

**Solutions:**
1. **Verify Contact Details**: Check your registered phone and email
2. **Check Settings**: Review notification preferences
3. **Test Notifications**: Ask school to send test message
4. **Update Information**: Correct any outdated contact details

#### Child's Information Not Updating
**Possible Causes:**
- Information pending approval
- System synchronization delay
- Insufficient permissions

**Solutions:**
1. **Wait for Approval**: Some changes require administrative approval
2. **Refresh Page**: Reload the page to see updates
3. **Contact School**: Verify if changes were received
