## Getting Started

### Logging In

1. Navigate to your school's website admin area (usually `yourschool.com/wp-admin`)
2. Enter your teacher username and password provided by the administrator
3. Click "Log In"

**First Time Login:**
- You'll be prompted to change your password
- Complete your profile information
- Review your assigned classes

### Understanding Your Role

As a teacher in the School Management System, you have access to:
- **Your Assigned Classes**: View and manage students in your classes
- **Attendance Marking**: Record daily attendance for your classes
- **Timetable Management**: View and update your teaching schedule
- **Student Records**: Access academic information for your students
- **Communication Tools**: Send notices and messages to students and parents
- **Academic Reports**: Generate reports for your classes

### Navigation Overview

The teacher interface is simplified to focus on your daily tasks:
- **Dashboard**: Overview of your classes and today's schedule
- **My Classes**: Manage your assigned classes
- **Attendance**: Mark and view attendance records
- **Timetable**: Your teaching schedule
- **Students**: View student information for your classes
- **Messages**: Communication with parents and administration
- **Reports**: Generate academic reports

## Class Management

### Viewing Your Classes

1. Navigate to **My Classes** from the main menu
2. You'll see all classes assigned to you with:
   - Class name and grade level
   - Number of enrolled students
   - Current academic term
   - Your role (Class Teacher, Subject Teacher)

### Class Details

Click on any class to view detailed information:

#### Student List
- **Enrolled Students**: Complete list with photos
- **Student Details**: Basic information and contact details
- **Academic Status**: Current performance indicators
- **Attendance Summary**: Individual attendance records

#### Class Information
- **Class Capacity**: Current enrollment vs. maximum capacity
- **Academic Year**: Current academic year and term
- **Classroom**: Assigned room or location
- **Schedule**: When you teach this class

### Managing Class Information

#### Updating Class Details
1. Click on a class name
2. Select **Edit Class Information**
3. Update available fields:
   - Classroom assignment
   - Class notes
   - Special instructions

**Note**: Some information like student enrollment and capacity can only be changed by administrators.

#### Adding Class Notes
1. In the class details page
2. Scroll to **Class Notes** section
3. Add notes about:
   - Class behavior observations
   - Academic progress notes
   - Special requirements
   - Parent communication records

## Attendance Management

### Daily Attendance Marking

#### Quick Attendance
1. From your dashboard, click **Mark Attendance**
2. Select the class and current period
3. The system shows all enrolled students
4. For each student, select:
   - **Present** (✓)
   - **Absent** (✗)
   - **Late** (L)
   - **Excused** (E)

5. Add notes for absent or late students if needed
6. Click **Save Attendance**

#### Detailed Attendance
1. Navigate to **Attendance > Mark Attendance**
2. Select class and date
3. For each student, you can:
   - Mark attendance status
   - Add individual notes
   - Record arrival time for late students
   - Mark excused absences with reasons

### Viewing Attendance Records

#### Class Attendance Summary
1. Go to **Attendance > View Records**
2. Select your class
3. Choose date range
4. View attendance patterns:
   - Daily attendance rates
   - Individual student patterns
   - Frequent absentees
   - Punctuality records

#### Individual Student Attendance
1. In the attendance section, click on a student's name
2. View their complete attendance history:
   - Attendance percentage
   - Absence patterns
   - Late arrival frequency
   - Excused vs. unexcused absences

### Attendance Notifications

#### Automatic Parent Notifications
When you mark a student absent, the system automatically:
1. Sends SMS to parents (if configured)
2. Sends email notification
3. Records the notification in the system

#### Manual Notifications
1. In the attendance marking screen
2. Select students who are absent
3. Click **Notify Parents**
4. Add a custom message if needed
5. Send the notification

## Timetable Management

### Viewing Your Timetable

#### Weekly View
1. Navigate to **Timetable**
2. Your weekly schedule displays:
   - **Time Slots**: All periods throughout the week
   - **Classes**: Which class you teach in each slot
   - **Subjects**: What subject you're teaching
   - **Rooms**: Classroom assignments
   - **Free Periods**: Available time

#### Daily View
1. Click on any day to see detailed daily schedule
2. View additional information:
   - Lesson topics (if planned)
   - Special notes
   - Room changes
   - Student count for each class

### Managing Your Schedule

#### Requesting Changes
1. In your timetable, click on a time slot
2. Select **Request Change**
3. Specify:
   - Reason for change
   - Preferred alternative time
   - Duration of change (temporary/permanent)

4. Submit request to administration

#### Reporting Conflicts
If you notice scheduling conflicts:
1. Click on the conflicting time slot
2. Select **Report Conflict**
3. Describe the issue
4. Administration will resolve the conflict

### Lesson Planning Integration

#### Adding Lesson Notes
1. Click on any scheduled class period
2. Select **Add Lesson Notes**
3. Include:
   - Lesson topic
   - Learning objectives
   - Resources needed
   - Homework assignments

#### Viewing Lesson History
1. In your timetable, click **Lesson History**
2. Review past lessons:
   - Topics covered
   - Student participation
   - Assessment notes
   - Follow-up actions needed

## Student Records

### Accessing Student Information

#### Your Students Only
You can only view information for students in your assigned classes:
1. Navigate to **Students**
2. The list shows only your students
3. Use filters to find specific students:
   - By class
   - By name
   - By attendance status

#### Student Profiles
Click on any student to view their profile:

##### Academic Information
- **Current Grade**: Grade level and class
- **Admission Number**: Unique student identifier
- **Academic History**: Previous classes and performance
- **Current Subjects**: All subjects the student is taking

##### Personal Information
- **Basic Details**: Name, date of birth, address
- **Parent/Guardian Information**: Contact details
- **Medical Information**: Allergies, medical conditions
- **Emergency Contacts**: Alternative contact persons

##### Academic Performance
- **Attendance Record**: Attendance percentage and patterns
- **Assessment Results**: Test scores and grades (if available)
- **Behavioral Notes**: Conduct and behavior records
- **Parent Communication**: History of parent interactions

### Updating Student Records

#### What You Can Update
As a teacher, you can update:
- **Academic notes**: Performance observations
- **Behavioral records**: Conduct notes
- **Attendance records**: Mark attendance and add notes
- **Communication logs**: Record parent interactions

#### What Requires Administrator Access
- Personal information changes
- Class transfers
- Medical information updates
- Parent contact details

### Student Communication

#### Contacting Parents
1. From a student's profile, click **Contact Parent**
2. Choose communication method:
   - **SMS**: Send text message
   - **Email**: Send email message
   - **Phone**: View parent phone numbers

3. Select message type:
   - Academic progress
   - Behavioral concern
   - Attendance issue
   - General communication

4. Compose and send message

## Communication Tools

### Sending Messages

#### Class Announcements
1. Navigate to **Messages > New Message**
2. Select **Class Announcement**
3. Choose your class(es)
4. Compose your message
5. Select recipients:
   - Students only
   - Parents only
   - Both students and parents

6. Send immediately or schedule for later

#### Individual Messages
1. Go to **Messages > New Message**
2. Select **Individual Message**
3. Choose the recipient (student or parent)
4. Compose your message
5. Send the message

#### Emergency Notifications
For urgent communications:
1. Select **Emergency Message** type
2. This gives your message priority delivery
3. Recipients receive immediate notifications
4. Use only for genuine emergencies

### Managing Messages

#### Inbox
1. Navigate to **Messages > Inbox**
2. View received messages from:
   - Parents
   - Students
   - Administration
   - Other teachers

#### Sent Messages
1. Go to **Messages > Sent**
2. Review messages you've sent
3. Check delivery status
4. View replies

#### Message Templates
Create templates for common messages:
1. Go to **Messages > Templates**
2. Create templates for:
   - Homework reminders
   - Attendance concerns
   - Academic progress updates
   - Behavioral feedback

### Notice Board

#### Creating Notices
1. Navigate to **Messages > Notices**
2. Click **Create Notice**
3. Enter notice details:
   - **Title**: Clear, descriptive headline
   - **Content**: Full notice text
   - **Target Audience**: Select who should see it
   - **Priority**: Normal, High, or Urgent
   - **Expiry Date**: When notice should be removed

4. Attach files if needed
5. Publish the notice

#### Managing Your Notices
1. View all notices you've created
2. Edit or update existing notices
3. Check who has viewed your notices
4. Remove expired notices

## Troubleshooting

### Common Issues

#### Cannot Mark Attendance
**Possible Causes:**
- Not assigned to the class
- Attendance already marked for the period
- System maintenance in progress

**Solutions:**
1. Verify you're assigned to teach the class
2. Check if attendance was already marked
3. Contact administration if the issue persists

#### Students Not Appearing in Class List
**Possible Causes:**
- Student not enrolled in your class
- Student status changed to inactive
- System synchronization delay

**Solutions:**
1. Verify student enrollment with administration
2. Refresh the page
3. Check student status

#### Cannot Send Messages
**Possible Causes:**
- Insufficient permissions
- Parent contact information missing
- SMS service unavailable

**Solutions:**
1. Verify parent contact details
2. Try alternative communication method
3. Contact system administrator

#### Timetable Not Displaying
**Possible Causes:**
- Schedule not yet published
- Browser cache issues
- System updates in progress

**Solutions:**
1. Clear browser cache
2. Try different browser
3. Contact administration
